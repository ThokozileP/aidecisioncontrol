ABOUT

Why the Forum exists

AI governance has made real progress on principles, responsibilities and risk frameworks. This is about what happens at the moment an AI-supported decision or action actually occurs.

POLICYAUTHORITYCONTEXTCONTROLEVIDENCEACCOUNTABILITY

How are those requirements enforced at the moment an AI-supported decision or action occurs?

As AI becomes embedded in operational workflows, organisations face a question governance alone doesn't answer. The AI Decision Control Forum exists to bring together the disciplines required to work through it, across engineering, risk, healthcare, financial services, assurance, regulation and research.

OUR FOCUS

Two moments, six questions.

BEFORE THE DECISION

Policy

What should be allowed, as a matter of organisational rule.

Authority

Who is entitled to decide, and on what basis.

Context

Under what circumstances the rule and the authority actually apply.

AT AND AFTER THE DECISION

Control

What happens before execution: what's checked, and what can stop it.

Evidence

What gets recorded at the moment the decision is made.

Accountability

Whether the organisation can explain and reconstruct the decision afterward.

WHO'S BEHIND IT

Convened by a practitioner, not a committee.

TP

Thokozile Phiri

Founder, AI Decision Control Forum

Thokozile Phiri founded the AI Decision Control Forum alongside Giggle AI Innovation and CARC (Clinical Agent Runtime Control), a runtime control and evidence layer for autonomous clinical AI agents. She is the publisher of the Decision Context Record™ framework, which defines the runtime evidence CARC's Evidence Engine captures for every clinical agent run (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20663952).

The Forum is an independent practitioner initiative, not a regulator, standards body or certification authority. Its purpose is to convene expertise, not to set rules.
