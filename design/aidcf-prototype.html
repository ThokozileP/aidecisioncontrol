THE OPERATIONAL GAP

Governance sets the rules. Decision control decides what happens next.

AI governance defines policy, risk classification and oversight. But once an AI system is acting inside a live workflow, a different question appears in real time: should this specific action be allowed to proceed, right now, in this context.

That operational layer, where policy becomes an enforceable runtime decision, is what this forum exists to work through.

01 · AUTHORISE

Is this permitted?

Is this AI system permitted to take this action, in this context, right now.

02 · CONSTRAIN

What boundaries apply?

What limits, thresholds or scopes bound this particular decision.

03 · ESCALATE

Who takes over?

When must authority move to a human or another control function.

04 · STOP

What must halt it?

Under what conditions must execution be prevented outright.

WHY THIS IS GETTING HARDER

The control problem changes as AI takes on more authority.

01

Models

Generate predictions. A human still decides what to do with them.

02

Assistants

Generate recommendations. A human still approves the action.

03

Agents

Initiate actions directly, inside defined boundaries.

04

Autonomous workflows

Coordinate decisions and execution end to end, with no human in the loop by default.
